Clipbot

A private desktop tool from the ViralClipzz clipping agency that turns livestreams we are licensed to clip into short vertical videos and publishes them to our own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What it does

  • Watches livestreams and long videos from creators who have authorized ViralClipzz to clip them.
  • Finds the most exciting moments and edits them into captioned vertical clips.
  • Uploads the clips to the ViralClipzz channels on YouTube (Shorts), TikTok and Instagram.

Use of Google and YouTube data

Clipbot uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the channel its owner has connected, and to read that channel's name so the owner can confirm which channel is connected. It does not access any other channel or any other user's data. See the Privacy Policy.

Clipbot is used only by ViralClipzz for its own channels. It is not a public service and does not offer sign-ups.
