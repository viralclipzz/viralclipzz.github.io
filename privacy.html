Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who we are

Clipbot is a private tool operated by ViralClipzz, a video clipping agency, for publishing clips to its own social media accounts.

Data we access

  • YouTube (Google): with the account owner's permission, Clipbot uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube.readonly (to read the connected channel's name). It does not read comments, subscribers, analytics or any other channel's data.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, avatar) and permission to upload or post videos to the connected account.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to the connected professional account and read their like and comment counts.

How we use it

Only to upload ViralClipzz's own clips to its own connected accounts and to measure how those posts perform. Clipbot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the ViralClipzz computer that runs Clipbot, in a file readable only by its owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Clips are processed with our video editing provider (Crayo) to create the videos.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. You can revoke Clipbot's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, to TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and to Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Revoking access stops all uploads; the local token file is then deleted.

YouTube Terms

By using Clipbot's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

We will update this page if Clipbot's use of data changes.
